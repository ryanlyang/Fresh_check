\section{Offline evaluation details}
\label{sec:offline-details}

\noindent\textbf{Draft: all numerical offline details in this appendix are
provisional placeholders from the 500,000-jet test, not the pending 20M
evaluation.} They support the single offline comparison in
Section~\ref{sec:offline-full-test-results} and will be replaced together
with its main tables.

The same-seed baseline comparisons use paired event identities. The current
placeholder 95\% class-stratified event-bootstrap intervals for selected
layerwise bias with EdgeValue minus the baseline
are $[0.8730,1.0202]$, $[1.0974,1.2454]$, and $[0.8542,1.0004]$
percentage points for replicas A, B, and C, respectively, in the same
order as Table~\ref{tab:offline-seeds}. Each excludes
zero for its fixed pair of trained checkpoints. These intervals do not
measure variability over new training seeds, and the original report does
not provide the analogous direct paired interval against the selected
layerwise model without EdgeValue.

The rejection placeholders use the same checkpoints and achieved efficiencies
of exactly 30\% and 50\% at the recorded operating points. SAT denotes at
least one seed with zero passing QCD jets; no finite-seed-only average is
substituted. For $H\to b\bar b$ at 30\%, the baseline QCD counts are
$(2,3,2)$ and selected EdgeValue counts are $(1,2,0)$. For
$t\to bq\bar q'$ at 30\%, they are $(0,2,1)$ and $(2,1,0)$,
respectively. For both lepton-containing classes, $H\to q\bar q\ell\nu$ and
$t\to b\ell\nu$, every checkpoint passes zero QCD events at both reported
efficiencies. These counts explain the saturated entries; they do not
establish zero underlying background probability.

The rejection comparison appears in the main text,
Tables~\ref{tab:offline-rejection-30} and~\ref{tab:offline-rejection-50}.

No rejection confidence intervals are stored in the placeholder source
metrics. Conditional Wilson intervals are pending from the official
full-test evaluation and must use that evaluation's passing counts and
background support, not the counts quoted here.
