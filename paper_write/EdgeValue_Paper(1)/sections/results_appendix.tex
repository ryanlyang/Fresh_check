\section{Complete numerical results}
\label{sec:complete-results}

Table~\ref{tab:overview} lists accuracy and macro AUROC for all eight
configurations, with differences measured against the shared-bias standard
ParT baseline. Tables~\ref{tab:rejection-benchmark} and
\ref{tab:rejection-30} give the complete class-wise rejection results at
the benchmark efficiencies and at 30\% signal efficiency, respectively.
The appendix configuration labels are defined in Appendix~\ref{sec:richer-setup}:
\texttt{Std} uses standard pair inputs, \texttt{Sel} adds PT, TRACK, and
REGION, \texttt{S}/\texttt{L} identifies shared/layerwise bias, and
\texttt{+EV} denotes edge-conditioned value messages. Every configuration
uses three independently trained models evaluated on the same 20M test
jets; the reported spreads are sample standard deviations across seeds.
